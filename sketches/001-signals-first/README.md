# Sketch 001 — Signals-first

## Design stance
Open with what *moved*, not what *is*. The top of the page answers "what do I need to know this morning" before any numbers.

## Key choices
- Layout: signal cards → glance metrics → benchmark + chart → top posts
- Signals are colored by direction (green up / red down / gray neutral) with a left border accent
- Sample-floor companies shown inline on the benchmark with a "floor" badge, not ranked
- Chart marks the outlier directly on the line

## Trade-offs
- Strong at: morning triage, the social-editor use case, CEO skim
- Weak at: someone looking for a specific metric has to scroll past the signals

## Best for
The 9am "did anything happen overnight" visit. Pairs with the objection-doc advice to lead with outliers, not ranks.
