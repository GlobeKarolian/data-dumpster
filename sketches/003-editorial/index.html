The Globe is holding the market, but WBUR is closing
Engagement up 11% on the window; one breakout post did more work than the entire gap to first place.
Morning briefing

The Boston Globe published 412 posts across nine platforms in the last 28 days, earning 5,182 engagements — up 11.2% on the prior window. WBUR holds the market lead at 5,739, a gap of 557 engagements.

That gap is smaller than a single post. The Globe's Bluesky story about a Cambridge cycling advocate earned 613 engagements, 38× the account's median and the strongest post in the market this window. GBH News is publishing 2.1× more than the prior window while holding its engagement rate at 0.42% — the best rate in the market. Boston.com and the Herald produced 127 and 48 engagements; both are below the sample floor and their ranks are not meaningful.
